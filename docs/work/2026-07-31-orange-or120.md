# Orange OR120 "Overdrive" — the fifth amp voice (M10.3)

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Branch:** claude/orange-or120-6f557i
**Roadmap item:** M10.3. Owner 2026-07-31: *"we are adding the orange soon… I'm an
orange man."* Owner also asked for the Rockerverb — that is **M10.7, a separate
slice**, deliberately not bundled (channel switching + master volume + its own reverb).

## Goal

The early-70s Orange OR120 picture-graphics head ships as the FIFTH selectable amp
voice (`orange`), web and native, with a synthesised Orange-style 4×12 cab to pair
with it — thick, midrange-forward, power-section breakup, no master volume.

## Approach

**Deliberate new voice, maximum reuse.** The OR120 is EL34 push-pull like the
JCM800, so `Jcm800Amp`'s power machinery (EL34 Koren fit, LTP PI with `tailRef`,
NFB/presence, the §55-style supply if warranted) is the starting point. **Research
first — there is NO spec in this repo.** I searched every branch, every ref's docs
tree and the full history: the only Orange content is the one-line roadmap entry.
Do the research as a first step and WRITE IT DOWN in §57 before coding.

What actually makes an OR120 an OR120, to be confirmed by research not assumed:

- **No master volume.** Like the Plexi, the power section is the overdrive. The
  breakup-onset story is therefore a VOLUME-knob story (the AC30 §46 precedent —
  onset must track the knob, and that is the testable property).
- **The unusual tone controls.** The OR120 has BASS / TREBLE plus the **"F.A.C."**
  (Frequency Analysing Control) stepped HP filter on some units, and a distinctive
  mid-forward voicing rather than the Marshall mid-scoop. The *difference from the
  JCM800's tone stack is the point of this amp* — if the stack ends up
  Marshall-shaped, the slice has failed regardless of what the tests say.
- **Thick, "woolly" low end** and strong midrange — the character report players
  give. Find the circuit reason (coupling caps, cathode bypass values, the stack),
  don't voice it with a fitted EQ curve.

**The cab: SYNTHESISE, do not download.** docs §15 built both existing cabs by
modal synthesis. A captured third-party PPC412 IR would break that pattern and drag
in provenance/licensing on a committed binary. Build an Orange-style 4×12 in the
same house style (`generateOrangeCab4x12IR` or equivalent), following how
`brit412` was made. If research says the PPC412's character is chiefly its
closed-back sealed cab + specific speaker, model that.

## How this will be measured

Breakup onset vs the VOLUME knob (§46 convention); the measured tone-stack
response vs its own analytic H(jω); **the mid-forward contrast against the JCM800
in dB at the relevant bands** — the number that proves this is an Orange and not a
re-skinned Marshall; THD curve; alias floor; the five existing goldens unchanged.

## Manual test steps

- [ ] Owner: pick `orange`, play at VOLUME ~5–7 — thick, midrange-forward, breaks
      up from the power section; A/B against the JCM800 at matched level and it
      should be obviously a different amp, not a nudge
- [ ] Orange cab vs brit412 on the same amp — audibly different, no fizz
- [ ] Edge: VOLUME 1.0 finite/bounded, reset clean, 44.1/96 k spot check

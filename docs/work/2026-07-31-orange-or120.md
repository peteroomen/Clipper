# Orange OR120 "Overdrive" — the fifth amp voice

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Branch:** claude/orange-or120-6f557i
**Roadmap item:** M10.3 — Orange tube-head style (ROADMAP.md:321)

## Goal

The early-70s Orange OR120 head ships as the fifth amp voice (`orange`) — core model,
C ABI, worklet, web UI, native engine + editor, assistant coaching — together with a
synthesised Orange-style 4×12 cab, and it is **measurably not a re-skinned JCM800**.

## Approach

There is **no OR120 spec anywhere in this repo** (one roadmap line), so step 1 is
research, written into `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` §57 with sources and an explicit list of
what could not be sourced, before any code.

The OR120 is EL34 push-pull like the 2204, so the EL34 Koren device law, the per-tube
plate-load Newton, the OT/bandwidth block, the rail+screen sag integrator and the
grid-blocking machinery are **reused** from `Jcm800PowerAmp`. Everything that makes an
Orange an Orange is a *circuit* difference, not an EQ curve:

1. **Cathodyne (split-load) phase inverter, not a long-tailed pair.** Sourced, and the
   single biggest sonic differentiator per the sources. Legs are anti-phase *by
   topology* (`Vplate = B+ − Vk`, `Vcathode = +Vk` off one current), so leg balance is
   1.000 by construction rather than by resistor selection, and the stage clips on
   **compliance** (Vk pinned between 0 and ~B+/2) — "stiffer, punchier, fuzzier".
2. **The driver triode is DC-coupled to the cathodyne** (sourced), so the driver plate
   node *is* the cathodyne grid node: they are solved jointly as one 3×3 Newton.
3. **Global NFB returns to the driver's cathode** (sourced), not to a second PI grid —
   a deeper loop, in front of the inverter. **HF DRIVE** is the presence control in
   that loop.
4. **James / passive-Baxandall tone stack (BASS + TREBLE, no MID)** instead of the
   Marshall FMV. This is the mid-forward mechanism: an FMV stack has a structural mid
   notch; a James network is two *parallel* shelving branches that leave the midrange
   alone and is flat at noon. Mid-forwardness is therefore **topological**.
5. **F.A.C.** — the six-position rotary is a series coupling cap into the stack, i.e. a
   real high-pass whose corner walks up as you click right (bass and gain come off).
6. **No master volume** → the power section is the overdrive, and breakup onset must
   track the VOLUME knob (the §46 AC30 convention).
7. **No bright cap on the volume pot** (the 2204 has a 470 pF one, docs §47) — a
   circuit reason for the thicker low end.

This is a NEW voice: nothing existing changes behaviour, so all five goldens must be
UNCHANGED (the scope check) and no bless is needed.

## How this will be measured

- **The bar:** mid-forward contrast vs the JCM800, in dB, as a hard assert — both at
  the tone-network level (`H(jω)` at noon: notch depth over 300–800 Hz relative to the
  mean of 100 Hz and 4 kHz) and on the **composed amps** (band energies of a rendered
  multi-tone). If the stack comes out Marshall-shaped the slice has failed.
- DC operating points: every triode 0.5–0.9 mA, plates 70–85 % of B+; EL34 quiescent
  dissipation under the 25 W plate rating.
- Cathodyne leg balance (must be ~1.000 by topology, not by fitting).
- Breakup onset (THD ≥ 5 %) tracking the VOLUME knob; THD monotone in VOLUME.
- Alias floor at 4× (project bar −60 dB), DC offset **on signal**, `--golden-report`.
- Perturbation proof for every key bar.

## What actually happened

**Step 1 was research, and the research channel turned out to be almost closed.** No host
outside the search tool was reachable: `WebFetch` returned HTTP 403 for *every* URL tried
(including `example.com`), and `curl` reported `CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403` /
"Host not in allowlist" for Prowess Amplifiers, the Orange Field Guide, SchematicHeaven,
the Orange forum and Wikipedia alike. So **no OR120 schematic was ever read**, and the
slice's first duty became making that unmistakable rather than papering over it. What IS
sourced (topology, plus exactly one component value — the early amps' 1500 pF treble cap)
and what is reconstruction are tabulated in docs §57.1.

The sourced topology turned out to be a gift: the OR120's differences from the 2204 are
structural, so the "not a re-skinned JCM800" bar could be met with circuit facts rather
than an EQ curve. The cathodyne phase inverter is the headline (players say so, and the
model gets exactly-balanced anti-phase legs for free where the JCM's LTP needed audit
finding 8 and a resistor sweep), and the James/passive-Baxandall stack is the mid-forward
mechanism: two parallel shelving branches that leave the midrange standing where an FMV
stack notches it.

Three of the plan's own premises were refuted by measurement and are reported, not fitted
away — see docs §57.9. The largest: **`kInterstageScale` does not set the breakup onset**
(it sat at VOLUME 0.59 across a 5× sweep, because down there the PREAMP clips first), so
the constant was derived against an absolute criterion instead — the smallest value at
which a cranked OR120 reaches its rated 120 W.

## Measured results

Full tables in docs §57. Headlines:

* **THE BAR** — mid-notch metric at noon: Orange James **+2.32 dB** (a bump) vs Marshall
  FMV **−6.03 dB** (a scoop) = **8.35 dB** of network contrast; on the composed amps
  **+1.15** vs **−5.09** = **6.24 dB**. Shipped bars 6.0 / 4.0, margins recorded not snugged.
* **Cathodyne**: leg balance **1.000000** (topological), driver gain −57.244, split-load
  gain **+0.9763**, plate+cathode ≡ B+c to 1e-9, compliance clip **−132.7 / +67.3 V**
  (asymmetric by construction).
* **DC**: V1A/V1B 1.343 mA at 58.4 % of B+; driver **0.627 mA**, cathodyne **0.737 mA**
  (both inside the project's 0.5–0.9 mA window); EL34 quad **33.31 mA / 16.64 W = 67 %** of
  the 25 W rating; rail 499.73 V.
* **Power**: cranked secondary **45.65 V peak = 130.2 W into 8 Ω** (rated 120), which is
  what derives `kFullScaleSecV = 50.7` (cranked peak measures **0.9004**).
* **Breakup tracks VOLUME**: ≥5 % THD onset at **0.50**; 0.76 % clean end, 47.29 % cranked;
  THD and RMS monotonic.
* **F.A.C.**: **17.21 dB** of low E across the six positions, monotone in both level and tilt.
* **NFB**: loop depth **7.81 dB**; **HF DRIVE** moves the 5 kHz-vs-220 Hz tilt by **+6.80 dB**.
* **Aliasing** (composed, cranked, 4186 Hz / 0.3 V): 4× **−67.1 dB at 48 k**, **−59.2 at
  44.1 k** (1× −10.2 / −11.3). Bar −56.
* **DC on signal**: 0.126 % of peak, with and without +0.1 V of input offset.
* **Cab**: −6 dB low corner **63.4 Hz** vs the Brit's 72.6; 1.2 kHz-minus-200 Hz **+3.29 dB**
  vs the Brit's −1.29; spectral peak 1.000000.
* **Goldens**: all five **UNCHANGED at ±0.00 dB**. Nothing blessed, nothing needed to be.
* **Perturbation**: 10 patches, all RED, suite GREEN restored — including P10 (bass pot
  1 M → 10 k) which trips the mid-forward bar itself at +0.58 dB.

# Dyna Comp / Ross compressor — the first dynamics pedal (M13.2)

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Branch:** claude/dyna-comp-6f557i
**Roadmap item:** M13.2. Owner 2026-07-31 asked for "the first two types" — OTA
**and** optical. This slice is the **OTA** one; the optical LA-2A-style voice is
**M13.3, a separate slice** following the SD-1/TS precedent (shared engine, two
voicings, two PRs).

## Goal

An MXR Dyna Comp / Ross-style compressor pedal (`comp`) ships web and native:
CA3080 OTA gain cell, two knobs (SUSTAIN + LEVEL), the squishy always-on pedal
compressor that country, funk and clean-lead playing need.

## Approach

**New effect family — the first dynamics processor.** Build it as a
**config-parameterized engine from the start**, the way `OverdriveEngine` serves
both the SD-1 and the TS: M13.3's optical voice will reuse it, and retro-fitting
that is more expensive than designing for it now. Name the split explicitly in
the header so the next slice does not have to guess the seam.

What must be modelled rather than approximated:

- **The OTA gain cell (CA3080).** The Dyna Comp's character is the *specific*
  control-current-to-gain law of the OTA, not a generic VCA. Research it and
  model it; a `gain = f(envelope)` curve pulled out of the air is the failure mode.
- **The detector time constants.** The Dyna Comp has **no attack or release
  knobs** — the constants ARE the pedal, so getting them right is most of the
  work. The famous "squish" and the equally famous attack-transient *loss* both
  come from here. Derive from component values.
- **The feed-forward vs feed-back topology.** Get this right from the schematic;
  it changes the whole behaviour and is frequently mis-stated in online writeups.

**Honest expectations to record, not fix:** a real Dyna Comp is noisy, squashes
attack, and colours the tone even at low settings. Those are the pedal, not bugs
— measure and report them. Do NOT add a "better" compressor behaviour that the
circuit does not have.

**The detector is also the noise gate's** (M13.6). Write it so that is possible,
and name it as a follow-up. **The wah slice running in parallel is also building
an envelope follower** — do NOT attempt to share code across in-flight branches;
record unification as a named follow-up instead.

## How this will be measured

The measured in/out transfer curve (ratio + knee) against the circuit's own
derivation; attack and release times from a step; **sustain extension in seconds
vs bypass**; transient loss in dB; noise floor at max SUSTAIN; five goldens
unchanged.

## Manual test steps

- [ ] Owner: add the comp in front of a clean Twin, SUSTAIN high — chords bloom
      and sustain, picking evens out; it should squish audibly, and the attack
      should soften (that is correct, not a defect)
- [ ] Country test: bridge pickup, SUSTAIN ~3 o'clock — chicken-pickin' pops
      should even out without the pedal sounding like it is pumping
- [ ] Edge: max SUSTAIN on silence — noise floor is audible but bounded and does
      not run away; fast knob moves = no zipper; reset clean; 44.1/96 k

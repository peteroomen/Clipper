# JCM800 Ra2 → 120 k — audit finding 8, the PI leg balance

**Date:** 2026-07-30
**Branch:** claude/amps-pedals-fixes-6f557i
**Roadmap item:** audit finding 8 (the last named circuit defect of the JCM cluster); slice 3 of the
field-report round — the even-harmonic "hair" behind "gain seems way too powerful"

## Goal

The JCM800 PI's legs balance (ratio ≥ 0.9 from today's 0.703), the push-pull's even-harmonic
cancellation becomes real (≥ 20 dB under the single-ended EL34 baseline), the two `finding8-*`
XFAILs XPASS and are deleted + hardened, and the NFB "meaningful loop" window is honestly
re-derived for the new plate pair.

## Approach

Deliberate tone change, small and targeted: `LtpInverter::Config::Ra2` 82 k → **120 k**
(`Jcm800PowerAmp.h`). The 82 k "asymmetric for balance" value was calibrated against the starved
pre-finding-7 inverter; with the tail reference honest, the diagnosis session's sweep (scratch
replica, digit-identical to shipped at 82 k) measured:

| Ra2 | legs | ratio |
|---|---|---|
| 82 k (shipped) | ×29.7/×20.9 | 0.703 |
| 100 k (the audit's guess) | ×28.7/×24.1 | 0.841 |
| **120 k** | ×27.6/×27.2 | **0.988** |
| 130 k | ×27.1/×28.7 | 0.944 |

DC targets stay inside the project windows (plates 76–78 % of B+, 0.72–0.76 mA). Effect: power-
section h2 at 0.2 V drive −33.2 → −37.8 dBc; full-amp THD floor at GAIN 0.2 drops 3.59 → 2.71 %,
at 0.5 13.2 → 10.7 %; output level +0.2–0.3 dB only. Owner chose 120 k + window re-derivation
over the 110 k compromise (AskUserQuestion, 2026-07-30).

**The known trap, planned for:** the NFB reduction rises with Ra2 (measured +0.37 dB at the
diagnosis probe) and the test's own probe sits at 5.977 of a < 6.0 ceiling — Ra2 = 120 k pushes
it over. The window is a measured-window guard, not physics (the physics is the adjacent
`|measured − 1/(1+βA)| < 1 dB` identity assert, which continues to hold); it is re-derived with
the new measured value + margin and a comment explaining what it forbids (token loop < 2 dB,
runaway loop) rather than loosened silently.

## What actually happened

As planned, with two honest surprises:

1. **The push-pull XFAIL did NOT XPASS.** Ra2 = 120 k moved the even-harmonic cancellation
   8.1 → 12.7 dB — real, but short of the 20 dB bar. Balanced small-signal legs are necessary
   but not sufficient; the XFAIL stays, re-owned to a future push-pull-residual investigation
   (candidates named in the decl: PI leg output impedances, the NFB injection's common-mode
   half, large-signal divergence). Only the leg-balance XFAIL was deleted and hardened.
2. **The composed 48 k alias floor moved** −58 → −54.7 dB (the balanced pair drives the EL34s
   ~1 dB harder at max-everything), tripping its −55 bar by 0.3 dB. Re-derived to −52 with the
   margin structure and escape hatch documented (oversample the PI if it drifts again). The
   aliasing printf now runs before its asserts so failures carry numbers.

The NFB window re-derivation went exactly as planned (6.37 measured vs 6.35 analytic; ceiling
6 → 8 with the derivation in the comment). Golden `rat_jcm800` moved only +0.14 dB RMS / 0.26 dB
worst band — inside the gates, **no bless needed**, drift documented in §45.

## Measured results

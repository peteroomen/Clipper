# AC30 dynamic sag — the low-end thickness (finding 4)

**Date:** 2026-07-31
**Branch:** claude/ac30-sag-6f557i
**Roadmap item:** audit finding 4 (the AC30 "sag" is a static saturator) + owner
feedback round 3, sharpened by the owner's Logic A/B (2026-07-31): "it's the low
end" — ours is thinner than the reference at high volume — "but the distortion
sounds better on our one than logic pros". Also from the feedback doc: high volume
has too much breakup past ~70–80, gets a bit fizzy at max.

## Goal

The AC30's power section gets a real dynamic supply (GZ34 rectifier impedance +
reservoir droop) and honest cathode-bias dynamics, so high-volume playing gains the
low-end thickness and touch-compression bloom the A/B found missing — while the
harmonic voicing the owner prefers over Logic's is PRESERVED (measured, not asserted).

## Approach

Deliberate fidelity change. The JCM/Twin already carry a measured dynamic rail model
(depth/bloom/recovery bars exist in their suites — reuse that machinery and its test
conventions rather than inventing new ones). The AC30's differences, from the real
circuit: a GZ34 VALVE rectifier (much higher source impedance than the Twin's
silicon — the classic Vox squash), class-A(ish) cathode-biased EL84s (average draw
high at idle; the signal-dependence of the draw differs from class-AB — derive from
the existing power-section model's plate current, don't assume AB behavior), and the
shared cathode-bias network whose bias point SHIFTS under sustained drive (the bloom).
Replace the static saturator with this dynamic pair; constants from the schematic
(GZ34 data, reservoir C, cathode R/C) with §42.6-style conventions for anything
re-derived.

PROTECTED PROPERTY (the owner's words): the high-volume harmonic voicing. Measure the
distortion spectrum (h2..h8 at VOLUME 0.6 and 0.85, matched drive) before/after — it
must move less than ~1 dB per harmonic EXCEPT where the sag's compression legitimately
reduces sustained-level THD; any larger movement needs the honesty-gate treatment
(report, don't fit). The fizz-at-max item is NOT this slice's target; re-measure the
§45-style alias floor at max volume and record whether the sag incidentally tames it.
